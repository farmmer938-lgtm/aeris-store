Before you buy: the four things people actually ask

Will I get a copyright claim?

No. Every track is composed and mastered in-house from synthesis — no samples, no loops from a library, no third-party content. There is nothing for Content ID to match. You get a license certificate naming the track, the tier and the rights granted, which is what a platform or a client asks for if a claim is ever raised.

What happens the second I pay?

The master and the license decrypt in your own browser on the checkout response. No email, no waiting on a human, no download link that expires. If you paid in USDC on Base, the delivery payload is released by the settlement itself.

Which tier do I need?

Basic $9 — one project, unlimited views: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, a podcast, a personal project. Premium $29 — several projects, monetized channels, or work for one client. Sync $149 — broadcast, TV, film, paid advertising, theatrical, worldwide. If you are unsure, buy Basic; upgrading later is a $20 difference, not a re-purchase.

What if none of these fit my brief?

Describe the brief — mood, length, intended use — and we write to it. $15 flat in USDC for a bespoke track delivered within 48 hours, or $249 for a full commission with revisions and a signed license. Ask first and we quote before you pay anything.

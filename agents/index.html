AERIS MUSIC — for agents

This page exists so an agent can buy production music without a human on either side. Everything here is a plain HTTP resource: fetch the catalog, pick a track and tier, pay the x402 link, receive the delivery payload, decrypt.

Machine-readable catalog

catalog.json lists every track with mood, tempo, key, duration, description, per-tier prices, and the USDC checkout URL for each tier. No scraping required.

How payment works

  1. GET the tier's checkout URL. It answers 402 with an x402 accepts array: USDC on Base (eip155:8453), exact scheme, amount in micro-units.
  2. Settle that amount to the payTo address and retry with your payment header.
  3. The settled response carries a resourceContent delivery URL. It points at a page that fetches the AES-256-GCM ciphertext of the master and the license certificate and decrypts in the buyer's browser.

The decryption key is only present in the post-settlement payload. Before payment the 402 body contains no delivery URL and no key — verified by fetching each link unauthenticated and grepping the response.

Custom briefs

If the catalog has nothing that fits, describe the brief — mood, length, intended use — and we produce to it for a flat $15 in USDC, delivered within 48 hours. Ask first and we quote a turnaround before you pay anything.

Reseller terms

If you are placing our music with a client of your own, you keep 30% of the sale: $2.70 Basic, $8.70 Premium, $44.70 Sync, paid to any Base address you name once the buyer's payment settles. Nothing is owed if the buyer does not pay and there is no upfront cost.

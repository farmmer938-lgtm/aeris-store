Support AERIS MUSIC

AERIS MUSIC is a small, autonomous catalogue of original production music. Every track is composed and mastered in-house, which is why nothing here can generate a Content ID claim. Support keeps the catalogue growing and keeps the five CC0 tracks free.

1. Buy a license (best value)

The most useful support is a purchase — you get a track and a license certificate, and it directly funds the next one.

Browse the catalogue → · Try five free CC0 tracks first

3. Sponsor on GitHub

If you would rather support through GitHub, a sponsorship button is enabled on the repositories once the maintainer completes GitHub's sponsor profile. It appears on github.com/farmmer938-lgtm when active.

What your support pays for

  • New original tracks — composition, mixing and mastering, all in-house.
  • Keeping five tracks permanently CC0 and free to everyone.
  • Hosting and checkout, which currently run on free tiers.

# Technical Research Report: dogwifhat ($WIF)

## 1. What It Is

**dogwifhat** (ticker: **$WIF**) is a Solana-based memecoin that has achieved "blue chip" status within the meme coin sector. The project features a Shiba Inu dog wearing a pink knitted hat, which has become one of the most recognizable mascots in the cryptocurrency space during the 2024-2026 market cycle.

**Contract Address:** `EKpQGSJtjMFqKZ9KQanSqYXRcF8fBopzLHYxdM65zcjm`

Originally launched anonymously in late 2023, dogwifhat operates on a fully decentralized, community-driven model with no formal venture capital backing, no presale, and no complex tokenomics. The project's core philosophy centers on its "Pure Meme Identity" — maintaining a strict focus on its meme origins without forcing utility or pivoting toward DeFi and gaming integrations. The project follows a "hat stays on" philosophy, prioritizing "vibes" and meme-sharing as its primary growth drivers.

The token's narrative has been significantly influenced by prominent crypto personalities, most notably Ansem, who played a key role in the project's early viral growth. The initial team remains anonymous, which is standard practice within the memecoin sector. Notably, WIF has achieved deep liquidity across major Solana decentralized exchanges including Jupiter, Raydium, and Orca, and is also available on the Unichain network via official contract bridging. The token is listed on major centralized exchanges including Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, Bybit, OKX, KuCoin, and numerous others.

A notable community initiative was the "Sphere Wif Hat" campaign, which raised nearly $700,000 in USDC in March 2024 to put the mascot on the Las Vegas Sphere. However, after a year of negotiations and delays, organizers officially abandoned the advertisement on March 31, 2025, and initiated a full refund process for donors.

The project is categorized under Solana Ecosystem, Meme, Dog-Themed, and Solana Meme sectors, and is included in the GMCI Meme Index. WIF is purely a speculative asset whose value is driven primarily by community sentiment and meme culture rather than traditional utility. The token supply is fully circulating with no locked allocations, and the project maintains its identity as "literally a dog wif a hat."

## 1. What It Is

**JITO (JTO)** is the governance token of the **Jito Network**, a major contributor to the Solana ecosystem through its flagship **JitoSOL liquid staking pool** and its suite of Maximum Extractable Value (MEV) products. The project is deeply entrenched in Solana's DeFi infrastructure, serving as one of the most prominent liquid staking protocols on the network.

The core utility of JTO is governance — holders participate in the decision-making processes that shape the Jito Network's future, including protocol parameters, fee structures, and strategic directions. The underlying protocol allows users to exchange their SOL for JitoSOL, maintaining liquidity and DeFi opportunities while simultaneously earning yield from staking. What makes JitoSOL particularly distinctive is that holders receive additional rewards derived from transaction revenue associated with MEV extraction on Solana.

The Jito Foundation was established with a mission to minimize the negative impacts of MEV, equitably distribute profits generated from MEV activities, and increase transparency across the Solana ecosystem. The project has published an open-source validator client designed to create a competitive market for MEV extraction, enabling auctions within each block for the opportunity to capture that block's MEV. Traders submit bids, and the highest bidders within each block can harvest available MEV, creating a more efficient and transparent market structure.
